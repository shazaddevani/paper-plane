# Privacy Policy — Paper Plane

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Paper Plane is a single-player game by Shazad Devani.

## What the app stores
Progress lives only on your device: coins, level, unlocked planes, stickers, and daily streak. That data is saved in on-device storage. It is not sent to us.

## What the app does not do
- No account, no login, no email collection
- No ads
- No in-app purchases
- No tracking across other apps
- No sale of personal information
- No analytics SDKs that identify you

## Updates
The iOS app may download game updates from Expo’s update service so you get fixes without a full App Store release. That connection is only for delivering the game. It is not used to build an advertising profile.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Contact
For privacy questions: use the email on your App Store Connect account (the seller email for Paper Plane).

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
