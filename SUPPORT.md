# Support — Paper Plane

Paper Plane is a backyard paper-dart game for iPhone.

**How to play:** Tap to throw. Hold to climb. Hold longer to loop back and fly the other way. Pull down to dive. Let go to glide. Clear every balloon to finish a yard.

**Progress:** Saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your planes and streak.

**Contact:** Use the seller email on the App Store listing.
